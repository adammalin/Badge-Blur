## Intent

Create a polished, approachable tutorial that shows a real fresh installation
of Badge Blur v0.22.1 and a complete first batch. The viewer should finish
knowing where to click, how to inspect and correct badge masks, and how to
export the finished images without needing additional instruction.

## Assets

- ../../demo-test-images — cleared synthetic source images for the fresh tutorial batch.
- ../../public/badge-blur.png — Badge Blur product mark for the opening and close.
- Actual Terminal and Badge Blur window recordings — the evidence layer for every procedural step.

## Customizations

- Smooth editorial zoom-ins that follow the current control or mask.
- Designed on-screen step labels, concise captions, and focus frames.
- Calm AI narration with a soft, unobtrusive instrumental music bed.
- Restrained interface sound cues at major transitions and confirmations.
- Before/After comparison and a clearly labeled manual-correction example if
  the automatic masks require no genuine repair.

## Notes

- Record only clean Terminal and Badge Blur window regions; never show the
  general desktop, notifications, unrelated files, or private paths.
- Install into an isolated tutorial directory and copy the synthetic images
  into a fresh source folder.
- Keep Badge Blur's green, charcoal, and soft-neutral visual language with
  rounded panels and readable high-contrast type.
- Human review remains part of the workflow; do not imply unattended
  compliance or perfect automatic detection.
