Mask editing help

Strong badge edges are fitted automatically after detection; uncertain results remain rectangles. Click a mask to reveal its four handles, then drag any corner to correct it. Drag across a missed badge to add a mask. Click the red ×, press Delete, or use Remove in the selected badge inspector to discard one.
