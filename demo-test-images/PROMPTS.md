# Generation prompts

All five images used the built-in image-generation tool and the
`photorealistic-natural` use case. Each prompt required fictional people,
fictional organizations, unreadable placeholder badge text, non-scannable
code-like graphics, and no ORNL, DOE, government, or real company marks.

## 01 — Single frontal badge

Create a realistic documentary-style photo of one fictional adult employee
walking through a generic modern research-office corridor, wearing a plain blue
lanyard with a clearly visible rectangular plastic photo ID badge centered on
the upper chest. Use vertical waist-up framing and soft overhead office
lighting. The badge should contain an invented portrait, abstract colored bands,
tiny unreadable placeholder text, and a fake non-scannable geometric code.

## 02 — Two-person event badges

Create a realistic candid photo of two fictional adults talking at an indoor
professional conference reception. Both wear lanyards and rectangular photo
badges. One badge faces the camera at an angle; the other is turned slightly
sideways. Use horizontal medium framing, mixed warm event lighting, and mild
background bokeh.

## 03 — Four-person group badges

Create a realistic group photo of four diverse fictional adult coworkers in a
generic laboratory lobby. Every person wears a lanyard and rectangular
identification badge, with natural variation in badge position and partial
occlusion. Use horizontal knees-up framing and bright diffuse institutional
lighting.

## 04 — Outdoor glare and motion

Create a realistic outdoor editorial photo of one fictional adult staff member
wearing a lanyard and rectangular photo badge while walking between generic
campus buildings. The badge should be rotated, catch bright glare, and show
slight motion while remaining identifiable. Use horizontal three-quarter-body
framing and hard midday sunlight.

## 05 — Low-light multiscale badges

Create a realistic wide candid photo in a dim auditorium lobby showing three
fictional adults wearing lanyards and rectangular badges. Place one person close
to camera, one at mid-distance, and one in the background. Use low mixed
lighting, mild high-ISO grain, and foreground/midground/background depth so the
badges appear at different scales.
